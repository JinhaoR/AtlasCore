Atlas 0.2.0 locally bundles Cormorant Garamond and Source Sans 3 under the SIL
Open Font License 1.1. Original copyright and full license texts are included at:
ui/fonts/Cormorant-Garamond-OFL.txt
ui/fonts/Source-Sans-3-OFL.txt
Source URLs, retrieval date and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in ui/fonts/sources.json.
Upstream projects: https://github.com/CatharsisFonts/Cormorant
and https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-sans
These files are served only from the extension; no runtime font service is used.

The Atlas Firefox UI bundles tldts and tldts-core 7.4.16.
Source: https://github.com/remusao/tldts
